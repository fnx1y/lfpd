01 / COMMUNITY

Why communities work better together

The best communities are not built by one person. They are built through hundreds of small interactions between people who choose to participate.

That can be something as simple as helping someone find the right channel, joining a training, answering a question, or making room for someone new to take part.

Nobody has to know everyone. Nobody has to be involved in everything. A good community simply gives people different ways to participate and lets them find what works for them.

02 / TRAINING

Getting more out of training

Training is most useful when you approach it as a chance to learn rather than something to simply get through.

Pay attention to what is being explained, ask questions when something is unclear, and take the opportunity to learn from the people around you.

Nobody starts knowing everything. Being willing to learn is often more valuable than pretending you already know the answer.

03 / TEAMWORK

Good teamwork is not complicated

Teamwork does not require everyone to think exactly the same way. It does require people to communicate clearly and understand what they are trying to accomplish.

Listen when someone else is speaking. Keep people informed when something changes. If you make a mistake, fix it and move on.

The small things tend to matter most. A team that communicates well is usually much easier to work with than one where everyone is trying to do everything alone.
