& Values

Los Francisco Police Department The ideas behind the community

A strong community is built on more than titles and ranks. The way people participate, communicate, and treat one another shapes the experience for everyone.

The values below represent the kind of environment the LFPD community aims to encourage.

01

Teamwork

Police roleplay is a community experience. Working with others, communicating clearly, and contributing to the people around you makes the experience better for everyone.

02

Respect

Everyone involved in the community deserves to be treated with respect. Good communication starts with understanding that there are real people behind the usernames.

03

Participation

A community is at its best when people actually get involved. Whether that means attending training, joining activities, or simply being part of the community, participation matters.

04

Improvement

Nobody knows everything from the beginning. Learning, asking questions, and improving over time are all part of becoming a stronger member of the community.

THE POINT

Make your time here
count.

Whether you are just starting out or already have experience within the department, there is always another opportunity to learn, participate, and contribute.
