Rules

Last updated: 2026 Los Francisco Police Department
ON THIS PAGE General Rules Department Authority Trainings & Sessions Roleplay Standards Conduct Enforcement

Being part of the Los Francisco Police Department means being part of an organised community. These rules help keep department activities consistent, fair, and enjoyable for everyone involved.

Members are expected to understand the rules that apply to them and to follow reasonable instructions given during official department activities.

General rules

Members should conduct themselves in a way that reflects well on the department, both inside and outside of official activities.

  • Follow the instructions of authorised department staff during official activities.
  • Do not intentionally disrupt department operations.
  • Do not abuse permissions, tools, or privileges provided to you.
  • Do not impersonate department staff or claim authority you do not have.
  • Do not deliberately exploit mistakes in systems, games, or department processes.

Trainings and official sessions

Trainings, cadet sessions, and other official activities work best when everyone involved allows the person hosting the session to do their job.

When attending a session, follow the instructions of the instructor or host. If you have a question, wait for an appropriate opportunity to ask it rather than repeatedly interrupting the session.

Members who repeatedly disrupt official activities may be removed from the session or face further action depending on the situation.

Roleplay standards

The purpose of roleplay is to create an enjoyable experience for everyone involved. Members should make a reasonable effort to participate in the scenario rather than intentionally turning it into chaos.

  • Respect ongoing roleplay situations.
  • Avoid deliberately ruining scenarios for other players.
  • Follow the instructions of the person leading an official roleplay activity.
  • Do not use department authority simply to inconvenience other players.

Member conduct

Rank and position come with responsibility. Members are expected to treat others fairly and to avoid using their position to intimidate, target, or unfairly punish others.

Concerns about another member should be reported through the appropriate channels rather than turned into public arguments or personal conflicts.

Enforcement

Rules may be enforced by authorised members of the department. The appropriate response depends on the circumstances and seriousness of the situation.

If you believe a decision was made incorrectly, use the appropriate department process to raise the issue. Public arguments rarely solve the original problem.

The goal of enforcement is to keep department activities functional and fair, not to make the community unnecessarily difficult to participate in.
