00 — INTRODUCTION

Community Rules

Adherence to the Los Francisco Police Department's regulations is mandatory for all community members at all times.

By joining and participating in the LFPD community, you agree to follow the rules and standards outlined on this page. Breaches of these rules may result in moderation or disciplinary action depending on the severity and circumstances of the violation.

01 — CHATTING

Communication Standards

All users are expected to communicate respectfully and appropriately while participating in the LFPD community.

  • 1.1 — All users are expected to respect each other while on our server.
  • 1.2 — Please speak in English while on our server.
  • 1.3 — Please put all content in its designated channel.
01.4 — SPAMMING

Spamming

Spam disrupts the community and makes channels difficult to use. The following forms of spam are prohibited:

  • Text spam
  • Image or file spam
  • Mention spam
  • DM spam
01.5 — FILES & LINKS

Suspicious or Inappropriate Content

Content that presents a security, safety, or community risk is not permitted.

  • Masked links, including keyloggers, IP loggers, or similar malicious links.
  • Pornography or sexually explicit content.
  • Unsolicited invite links or advertisements.
  • Disturbing or upsetting content, including gore, violence, or abuse.
01.6 — CONVERSATIONS

Inappropriate Conversations

Members are expected to exercise judgment when discussing sensitive subjects and interacting with others.

  • Controversial or sensitive topics, including religion and political views, should not be used to create conflict.
  • Racism, sexism, transphobia, homophobia, and harassment are prohibited.
  • Banter is permitted within reason and must not become targeted harassment.
  • Civil arguments are permitted but may not be used as a means to demean others.
01.7 — HARASSMENT

Provoking, Targeting & Harassment

Any behavior intended to repeatedly target, intimidate, demean, or negatively affect another member is prohibited.

  • Actions intended to make a user feel negatively about themselves, their friends, or their family.
  • Negatively targeting users through direct messages or repeated negative remarks.
  • Constantly pinging or contacting users in a disruptive or hostile manner.
  • Bullying.
  • Encouraging suicide or self-harm.
  • Sexism.
  • Racist, homophobic, or transphobic conduct is not permitted under any circumstances.
01.8 — DISCORD COMPLIANCE

Discord Terms & Guidelines

As a Discord server, the LFPD community is bound by Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. All members are expected to familiarize themselves with and follow those requirements in addition to the rules listed here.

Failure to follow Discord's policies may result in action from Discord and may also result in moderation within the LFPD community.

Discord Terms of Service Discord Community Guidelines
01.9 — STAFF AUTHORITY

Authority of Staff Members

At any time, members are expected to comply and cooperate with Moderators and Administrators when asked to do so in relation to community management or moderation.

If you have questions about a moderation action or community rule, please contact a member of the moderation team.

02.0 — VOICE CHAT

Voice Chat Guidelines

  • Refrain from using excessively loud or jarring audio.
  • Avoid using soundboards during critical moments or when they disrupt conversations.
  • Maintain professional and appropriate communication.
