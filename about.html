About the
LFPD

Los Francisco Police Department A place to learn, play, and get involved

The Los Francisco Police Department is a Roblox police roleplay community built around participation, training, and community.

Whether you are interested in joining the department, taking part in roleplay, attending training, or simply finding a community to be part of, LFPD provides different ways to get involved.

LFPD
01

A place to participate

There is more to a department than simply having a rank. Members can take part in activities, learn from others, and find different ways to contribute to the community.

02

Built around people

The community is made up of the people who choose to participate in it. Communication, teamwork, and a willingness to get involved all play a part in the experience.

03

Different ways to get involved

From the main department to specialised divisions, members can explore different parts of the LFPD community and find what interests them.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Come for the roleplay.
Stay for the community.

Getting involved does not have to mean knowing everything from the start. Everyone begins somewhere.

The best way to learn about the department is to explore, ask questions, and take part in the opportunities available to you.
