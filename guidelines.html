Guidelines.

01 — RESPECT

Treat others with respect.

The LFPD community is built around the people who participate in it. Every member is expected to contribute to an environment where others can participate without being targeted, harassed, or demeaned.

Disagreements are a normal part of any community, but they must remain civil. Personal attacks, bullying, targeted harassment, and discriminatory behavior have no place within LFPD.

02 — COMMUNICATION

Communicate responsibly.

Members should communicate clearly and appropriately across the community.

  • Use English when communicating within the server.
  • Keep conversations appropriate for the channel being used.
  • Avoid unnecessary spam, excessive mentions, and repeated messages.
  • Keep disagreements civil and avoid using arguments as a way to demean others.
03 — CONTENT

Share content responsibly.

Content shared within the LFPD community should be appropriate, relevant, and safe for other members.

  • Do not share malicious, suspicious, or deceptive links.
  • Do not share pornography or sexually explicit content.
  • Do not share disturbing or upsetting content.
  • Do not send unsolicited advertisements or invite links.
04 — COMMUNITY CONDUCT

Help build the community.

Members are encouraged to contribute positively to the LFPD community and help maintain an environment where people can participate comfortably.

This includes respecting the boundaries of other members, avoiding unnecessary conflict, and considering how your actions may affect the wider community.

05 — STAFF COOPERATION

Cooperate with staff.

Moderators and Administrators are responsible for helping maintain the community. Members are expected to cooperate with reasonable staff requests relating to moderation, safety, and the enforcement of community standards.

If you believe a moderation decision was made incorrectly, raise your concerns respectfully through the appropriate channels rather than escalating conflict publicly.

06 — VOICE CHAT

Keep voice chat comfortable.

  • Avoid excessively loud or jarring audio.
  • Avoid disruptive soundboards during important conversations.
  • Maintain appropriate and respectful communication.
  • Be considerate of everyone participating in the channel.
FINAL NOTE

Be the kind of member you want around.

A strong community is created by the people who choose to participate in it. Respect others, communicate responsibly, and help make LFPD a community worth being part of.
