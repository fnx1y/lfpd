LOS FRANCISCO POLICE DEPARTMENT CHAIN OF COMMAND
RANK STRUCTURE

A clear chain of command.

The Los Francisco Police Department operates through a structured chain of command. Each rank carries its own responsibilities, authority, and place within the department.

CR

Chief Ranks

EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP
01

Chief of Police

CR

The highest-ranking authority within the Los Francisco Police Department.

02

Assistant Chief

CR

Assists the Chief of Police in overseeing the department and its operations.

03

Deputy Chief

CR

Supports executive leadership and assists in the administration of the department.

HQ

Headquarters

DEPARTMENT HEADQUARTERS
04

Chief Inspector

HQ

Provides senior oversight and assists in the administration of major departmental operations.

05

Inspector

HQ

Oversees departmental operations and supports the leadership of assigned areas.

06

Deputy Inspector

HQ

Assists Inspectors and Headquarters leadership with departmental oversight and coordination.

HR

High Rank

SENIOR COMMAND
07

Chief Superintendent

HR

Provides senior leadership and oversight across the department.

08

Superintendent

HR

Oversees major areas of departmental activity and senior personnel.

09

Sheriff

HR

Holds senior command authority and assists with the direction of departmental operations.

10

Deputy Sheriff

HR

Supports senior command and assists in the supervision of departmental personnel.

MR

Mid Rank

COMMAND
11

Commander

MR

Provides command-level leadership and oversees departmental personnel and operations.

12

Colonel

MR

Holds senior command responsibilities and assists in directing departmental operations.

13

Major

MR

Supervises significant departmental functions and personnel.

14

Captain

MR

Leads assigned personnel and assists with the management of departmental operations.

LR

Low Rank

FIELD PERSONNEL
15

Lieutenant

LR

Supervises personnel and assists with the management of field operations.

16

Sergeant

LR

Supervises officers and supports the coordination of daily departmental activity.

17

Senior Corporal

LR

An experienced field rank providing support and leadership to other personnel.

18

Corporal

LR

Supports senior personnel and assists with the supervision of department members.

19

Senior Constable

LR

An experienced constable who demonstrates increased knowledge and responsibility.

20

Constable

LR

A trained member of the department responsible for participating in department operations.

21

Police Officer

LR

Performs general police duties and participates in department activities and roleplay.

22

Police Cadet

LR

An entry-level member beginning their journey within the Los Francisco Police Department.
