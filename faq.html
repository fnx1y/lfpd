Frequently
Asked Questions

Los Francisco Police Department A few things people commonly ask

New here? Start below. These answers cover some of the basics about joining and participating in the Los Francisco Police Department.

How do I join the Los Francisco Police Department?

The recruitment process begins by joining the LFPD community and waiting for an instructor to host a cadet training.

Visit the recruitment page for the current instructions.

Where do I go for cadet training?

Cadet training is organised through the LFPD community. You should join the game and wait for an instructor to host a cadet training.

Do I need to join the Discord server?

The LFPD Discord community is the best place to keep up with department information, communicate with other members, and find out when activities are taking place.

What divisions can I join?

LFPD has several divisions, including the Academy Protection Unit, Traffic Unit, Training & Education Unit, and Special Weapons & Tactics.

You can learn more about each division on the divisions page.

Do I have to join a division?

You do not need to join a division, however we recommend joining one for their special perks and fun events! More information can be found at the divisions tab.

Where can I find the department rank structure?

The complete rank structure is available on the ranks page.

What should I do if I have a question or issue?

The best place to start is the LFPD community. Depending on the situation, you can ask a question, speak with a member of the moderation staff, or open a ticket.
